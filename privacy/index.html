Privacy · last checked 15 October 2026

Almost nothing, and here's the almost.

No cookies, no mailing list, no forms, and nothing that asks who you are. What the site does record is below, all of it, with the reason, checked against the code and against Cloudflare's own documentation rather than written from memory.

  • Which pages get read

    Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, the page that sent you, and your country, device type, browser and operating system, so I can tell whether anybody reaches the case studies. It doesn't use cookies or anything stored in your browser, and Cloudflare says it doesn't fingerprint visitors by IP address, browser or anything else.

  • How fast the pages load

    The same Cloudflare script reports how long each page took to load and settle on your device, so I find out when a change has made the site slower. It isn't tied to you either.

  • A few choices, kept on your device

    Three things are kept in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time: ch-design, which of my two designs you picked, ch-poll, how you voted in the poll on the earlier designs page, and motion-preference, if you change the motion setting. Three more sit in session storage and go when you close the tab: ch-viewing, which design you're looking at so the inner pages are drawn in it, desk-hint, so a hint on the desk only shows once, and ch-vt, a note that carries the change of design from one page to the next. If you scroll to the inclusive.io app in its case study, it writes a few settings of its own, all starting inclusive-, for things like your theme and contrast choice. None of that is sent anywhere, apart from the poll vote described below, and clearing this site's data removes it. Nothing on this site sets a cookie.

  • What I'm listening to

    The tablet on the desk shows my Spotify, not yours, and nothing about you goes anywhere near it. It asks my own little server on Cloudflare what I'm playing, and the album art loads straight from Spotify's image server, which sees the request the way any image host does.

  • The poll on the earlier designs page

    If you vote, your browser sends my little server on Cloudflare which design you picked and, if you're changing your mind, the one you picked before, so it can move one count to the other. Nothing else goes with it: no name, no ID, nothing that says who you are. The server keeps two numbers, one for each design, and nothing about who voted.

  • The live apps in the case study

    The inclusive.io case study runs my app from this site, so it loads nothing from anywhere else. Its design system pages link out to CodeSandbox for the code examples, and only go there if you follow one.

  • If you write to me

    Email to craig@craighawkes.dev goes to a Google-hosted mailbox and I read it myself. There's no form on this site and no list to add you to.

  • Who's responsible, and what you can ask

    I am, Craig Hawkes, in Hamilton, Scotland. Email the address above to ask what's held about you or to have it removed. The analytics can't tell me who you are, so there's usually nothing to find, and I'll tell you that rather than go quiet. If you're not happy with the answer, the Information Commissioner's Office is at ico.org.uk.

  • Who else handles the request

    Cloudflare hosts the site on Cloudflare Pages, and like any host it handles the requests your browser makes. The little server behind the tablet and the poll runs on Cloudflare too. Cloudflare's own pages say what the analytics above collect, and they're where the detail on this page came from.
