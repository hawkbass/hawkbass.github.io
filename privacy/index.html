Privacy · last checked 23 September 2026

Almost nothing, and here's the almost.

No cookies, no mailing list, no forms, and nothing that asks who you are. What the site does record is below, all of it, with the reason, checked against the code and against Vercel's own documentation rather than written from memory.

  • Which pages get read

    Vercel Web Analytics counts page views, the page that sent you, and your country, device type, browser and operating system, so I can tell whether anybody reaches the case studies. It sets no cookies. Vercel tells one visitor from another with a hash made from the request, discards it after 24 hours, and doesn't tie any of it to an IP address.

  • How fast the pages load

    Vercel Speed Insights reports how quickly each page loaded and settled on your device, with your network speed, device type, browser and country, so I find out when a change has made the site slower. It isn't tied to you or to your IP address either.

  • One setting, kept on your device

    If you change the motion setting while Receipts is on, the choice is saved in your own browser's storage so it's still there next time. It never leaves your device, and clearing this site's data removes it. Nothing else is stored, and nothing on this site sets a cookie, the embedded apps included.

  • What I'm listening to

    The now-playing strip is my Spotify, not yours, and nothing about you goes anywhere near it. The album art loads straight from Spotify's image server, which sees the request the way any image host does.

  • The live apps in the case study

    The inclusive.io case study embeds two of my own apps, served by Vercel. They only load when you scroll down to them.

  • If you write to me

    Email to craig@craighawkes.dev goes to a Google-hosted mailbox and I read it myself. There's no form on this site and no list to add you to.

  • Who's responsible, and what you can ask

    I am, Craig Hawkes, in Hamilton, Scotland. Email the address above to ask what's held about you or to have it removed. The analytics can't tell me who you are, so there's usually nothing to find, and I'll tell you that rather than go quiet. If you're not happy with the answer, the Information Commissioner's Office is at ico.org.uk.

  • Who else handles the request

    Vercel hosts the site, and like any host it handles the requests your browser makes. Its documentation lists exactly what the analytics above collect, and it's where the detail on this page came from.
